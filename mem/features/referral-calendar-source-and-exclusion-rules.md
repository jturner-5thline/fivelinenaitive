name: Referral calendar source and exclusion rules
description: Authoritative calendar owners and attendee-domain exclusions for Meetings with Referral Sources
 type: feature
---
The Meetings with Referral Sources metric uses calendar events owned only by the approved team calendars: Chandler Minaldi, Flor Fustinoni, John Moffitt, James Turner, Niki Heikali, Paz Piña, Scott Williams, and Klawless. Exclude any event whose attendees include a funding-source domain or a client domain. Funding-source domains come from funding-source records and contacts; client domains come from all deal-linked client contact emails/additional emails, contact URLs, deal contact emails, deal/company URLs, and linked CRM company URLs. Calendar events may be enriched with matching Claap recordings/transcripts but calendar data remains authoritative.
